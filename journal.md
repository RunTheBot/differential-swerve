The FRC Season is over so I have too much time on hand. Lets make a diffy swerve!

## Research
I am basing my design on exsiting "Pod Archetypes". I mainly looked at VEXU COMET's [research](https://doi.org/10.31224/5757). I also already have assembled MK4i, MK4n, and MK5n swerve modules so I took inspo from there.

## Design
After ~1h of research I got started on design. I wanted to use a 2.5in colson but, VEX shut down their VEX Pro product line so no colsons and no 2.5 in. I opted for a 3in gobuilda wheel instead.
